Department Overview

Our Vision

To be a nationally recognized center of excellence in computing education, producing graduates who are innovative, ethical, and ready to lead in a rapidly evolving digital world.

Our Mission

We are committed to delivering a rigorous and inclusive Integrated B.Tech education that combines strong theoretical foundations with practical, real-world problem solving. We nurture curiosity, integrity, and collaborative innovation in every student who joins Sanjivani University.

As a quick technical welcome: did you know a single kilobyte is defined as 210 bytes, and one of the most famous chemical formulas you will revisit even in computing hardware labs is H2O? Precision with notation like this is a habit we build from day one.

Curriculum & Topics

Academic Stages

  1. First Year – Core Foundations (Programming, Discrete Math, Digital Logic)
  2. Second Year – Systems & Structures (Data Structures, Computer Architecture, OS)
  3. Third Year – Specialized Electives (AI, Networks, Databases, Security)
  4. Fourth Year – Capstone & Industry Practicum (Final Year Project, Internship)

Core Skills & Tools You'll Learn

  • Logic Building & Algorithmic Thinking
  • Interface Design (HTML, CSS, UI/UX Basics)
  • Database Basics (SQL & Relational Design)
  • Version Control with Git
  • Python & Java Programming
